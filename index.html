Bars / Restaurants

The 25 best pubs within roughly a 15-minute walk of our base at 26 Dublin Street Lane South. Ranked for atmosphere, Scottish character, beer, whisky and overall group potential.

1
Thistle Street Bar
Tiny, traditional Scottish pub with great cask ales, whisky and a genuinely local New Town atmosphere. One of the best places to start a proper pub crawl.
Thistle Street Bar ↗ Directions
2
Milnes Bar
Historic city-centre boozer with literary character, cask ales, Scottish whisky, sports and live music. A very good group pub.
Milnes Bar ↗ Directions
3
The Broughton
Warm, polished neighbourhood pub on Broughton Street with excellent food, perfectly poured pints and a strong overall group atmosphere.
The Broughton ↗ Directions
4
Mathers
Proper old-school Edinburgh boozer with a straightforward atmosphere, sports and affordable pints. A classic local rather than a tourist bar.
Mathers ↗ Directions
5
Cask & Barrel
Proper Scottish pub with real ale, whisky and a classic drinking-pub feel. One of the better choices when you want a no-nonsense pint.
Cask & Barrel ↗ Directions
6
The Keller
Tucked away down Broughton Street Lane, this cosy craft-beer taproom focuses on interesting beers and a relaxed, locals-focused atmosphere.
The Keller ↗ Directions
7
The Guildford Arms
One of Edinburgh's most beautiful historic pubs, with ornate Victorian interiors, real ale and a long family history dating back to 1896.
The Guildford Arms ↗ Directions
8
The Auld Hundred
One of Rose Street's oldest pubs, serving Scottish food, local drinks and more than 100 whiskies, with live music seven nights a week.
The Auld Hundred ↗ Directions
9
The Kenilworth
Historic Rose Street pub with distinctive old-world character, tiled interiors and a lively central location. Great for a traditional Edinburgh stop.
The Kenilworth ↗ Directions
10
The Abbotsford
Beautifully preserved Edwardian pub with an original mahogany island bar, ornate interior and upstairs restaurant. One of the city's great historic drinking rooms.
The Abbotsford ↗ Directions
11
Café Royal
Iconic Victorian oyster bar and restaurant with stunning interiors, seafood, beers and continental lagers. A great spot for a more upscale drink.
Café Royal ↗ Directions
12
Kay's Bar
A tiny, exceptionally preserved Victorian bar and one of Edinburgh's premier cask-ale pubs. This is an absolute must-visit for the trip.
Kay's Bar ↗ Directions
13
The Cumberland Bar
Classic Victorian New Town pub with wood-panelled surroundings, real ales, good food and a secluded beer garden. A great neighbourhood stop.
The Cumberland Bar ↗ Directions
14
The Black Cat
Small, lively Rose Street pub known for cask beers, an extensive whisky selection, live music and a friendly atmosphere.
The Black Cat ↗ Directions
15
Dirty Dick's
Quirky, old-school Rose Street pub with a deliberately cluttered interior, Scottish food, whisky and a fun, informal atmosphere.
Dirty Dick's ↗ Directions
16
Rose & Crown
Traditional Rose Street pub with live entertainment, sports, real ales and a lively group-friendly atmosphere right in the heart of New Town.
Rose & Crown ↗ Directions
17
Rose Street Brewery
Historic Rose Street pub with classic British pub food, cask ales, whisky and a lively central location. Good choice for food and drinks together.
Rose Street Brewery ↗ Directions
18
The Oxford Bar
Tiny, no-frills Edinburgh institution made famous by Inspector Rebus and Ian Rankin. Old-school, intimate and about as far from a tourist trap as you can get.
The Oxford Bar ↗ Directions
19
Barony Bar
Beautifully restored Broughton Street institution with Victorian details, mahogany, real ale, whisky and a strong gastro-pub offering.
Barony Bar ↗ Directions
20
Pickles
Small, highly regarded wine-and-food spot with a fun intimate atmosphere. More restaurant/bar than traditional pub, but an excellent group stop.
Pickles ↗ Directions
21
The Conan Doyle
Large traditional Edinburgh pub near York Place with plenty of room for groups, classic pub food, sports and a lively atmosphere.
The Conan Doyle ↗ Directions
22
Dublin Street Social
Very close to the house and ideal for food and drinks. Modern neighbourhood spot with small plates, good wine, cocktails and a lively downstairs bar.
Dublin Street Social ↗ Directions
23
Outhouse
Quirky hidden bar down Broughton Street Lane with a big beer garden, live music, cocktails and a relaxed group atmosphere.
Outhouse ↗ Directions
24
The Queens Arms
Hidden below Frederick Street with excellent Scottish food, cask ales, craft beer and whisky. Also a strong option for watching sports.
The Queens Arms ↗ Directions
25
Stockbridge Tap
Specialist real-ale pub with an excellent beer selection and knowledgeable staff. CAMRA Edinburgh Pub of the Year 2025 and a worthwhile walk from the house.
Stockbridge Tap ↗ Directions
